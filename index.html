Treatments

Therapy tailored to individual needs, drawing on traditions refined over millennia

01

Acupuncture

A traditional Chinese medicine technique using fine, sterile needles at specific acupoints to restore the body's natural energy flow, reduce pain, and promote healing.

02

Acupressure

An ancient Chinese self-healing system that uses targeted pressure on specific meridian points to relieve tension, improve circulation, and rejuvenate the body's natural vitality.

03

Marma Therapy

An ancient Ayurvedic practice stimulating 107 vital Marma points on the body to release blocked energy, relieve chronic pain, and restore deep harmony between body and mind.

04

Naturopathic Medicine

A holistic approach to healthcare that harnesses natural remedies, nutritional counselling, and lifestyle guidance to treat and prevent illness at its root cause.

Clinics in London and consultations worldwide — including remote telephone consultations — mean that compassionate, expert care is always within reach. Beyond treating symptoms, Mr. Shaikh works to uncover root causes, addressing physical pain and emotional imbalances with equal attentiveness.
