Dr Ali's Himalayan Retreat

Dr Ali's Himalayan Retreat is the truest form of Nature Cure Centre's philosophy: heal fully immersed in nature. Our simple four-bedroom cottage is set amidst lush hills, pine forest and terraced farmland. For over 30 years, our retreat has helped Europeans detox, rehabilitate, and heal naturally.

Each stay combines high-altitude walks, massage, and homecooked meals with locally sourced organic ingredients. Air Quality Index (AQI) averages 2 throughout the year, making this one of the most pristine places on Earth. Guests come to heal from post-COVID complications, reset from burnout, and overcome depression, anxiety and trauma.

Our Practitioner

Meet Nizam

Nizam is a Naturopath and Integrated Acupuncturist with two decades of dedicated practice. His approach integrates Traditional Chinese Medicine and Naturopathy with an understanding of each patient's unique journey.

Nizam's current clinical practice is in Central London in the heart of the U.K.'s medical district. With two decades of experience treating patients in New Delhi, Hong Kong, Italy, Mexico, and Oman, and qualifications in Integrated Acupuncture, Naturopathy and shockwave therapy, Nizam's work speaks for itself.

His specialties are detoxification, weight management, pain management, stroke rehabilitation, massage therapy, integrated acupuncture, cancer management (stage 1-2) with nutritional support, and depression.

PSA Accredited Naturopath Integrated Acupuncturist
