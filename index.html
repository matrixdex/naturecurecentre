Dr Ali's Himalayan Retreat

Dr Ali's Himalayan Retreat is the truest form of Nature Cure Centre's philosophy: heal fully immersed in nature. Our simple four-bedroom cottage is set amidst lush hills, pine forest and terraced farmland. For over 30 years, our retreat has helped Europeans detox, and heal naturally.

Our Practitioner

Meet Shaikh Nizamuddin

Shaikh Nizamuddin is a Naturopath and Integrated Acupuncturist with over three decades of dedicated practice. His approach integrates Traditional Chinese Medicine, Ayurveda and Naturopathy with an understanding of each patient's unique journey.

Shaikh Nizamuddin's current clinical practice is in London, UK. With over three decades of experience treating patients in New Delhi, Hong Kong, Italy, Mexico, and Oman, and qualifications in Integrated Acupuncture, Naturopathy and shockwave therapy, Shaikh Nizamuddin's work speaks for itself.

His specialties are detoxification, weight management, pain management, stroke rehabilitation, massage therapy, integrated acupuncture, and cancer management with nutritional support.
