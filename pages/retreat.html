The Retreat

Immersive healing, in Nature

Nature Cure Centre's residential retreat is set in the Kangra Valley of Himachal Pradesh, at the foot of the Dhauladhar mountains. Guests stay in a four-bedroom cottage surrounded by pine forest, terraced farmland, and lush hills. This pristine environment is part of our treatment.

Our Himalayan Retreat treats the whole by removing patients from all that keeps them unwell. An escape from noise, air and light pollution, deadlines, and the hustle and bustle of cities, with clean mountain air, unhurried days and simple organic food.

Stays are between 15 and 30 days. Our Himalayan Retreat is a sublime place to recover from burnout, depression, anxiety or trauma, and post-COVID complications. Gradually through repetition, the same walk every morning, the same quiet evenings, the same home-cooked meals, the nervous system begins to settle on its own by calming its fight-or-flight response.

What a Stay Involves

High-Altitude Walks

Guided daily walks on hillside trails for strength and lung recovery

Massage

Regular massage to release tension, manage pain and promote the body's innate healing

Homecooked Food

Simple vegetarian meals prepared fresh from local produce for naturopathic nutrition

Clean Mountain Air

Kangra Valley's pristine air is a rare, tangible form of relief for the body and mind

Who This Is For

Nature Cure Centre's Himalayan Retreat is a reset for anyone feeling stuck. Clinically, our retreat aids recovery for patients suffering from anxiety, depression, trauma, and post-COVID complications. We also offer long-term care for the elderly.
