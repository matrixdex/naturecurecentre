Traditional Chinese Medicine

Acupuncture

Acupuncture is one of the world's oldest and most respected healing traditions. By inserting fine, sterile needles into specific acupoints along the body's energy meridians, we stimulate the flow of Qi — the body's vital force — to restore balance and promote healing.

Used for over 3,000 years, acupuncture is now supported by a growing body of modern research confirming its effectiveness across a wide range of conditions.

Ancient Chinese Healing

Acupressure

Acupressure applies the same principles as acupuncture — working along the body's meridians and acupoints — but uses targeted fingertip pressure rather than needles. This makes it a gentle, accessible therapy suitable for all ages.

Developed alongside acupuncture in ancient China, acupressure is particularly effective for those seeking a non-invasive, deeply relaxing treatment that also carries therapeutic benefit.

✓
Relieves muscle tension and stiffness
✓
Promotes deep relaxation
✓
Eases headaches and migraines
✓
Improves circulation
✓
Suitable for all ages, including children

Marma Therapy

Marma Therapy is an ancient Ayurvedic practice that works with 107 vital energy points — called Marma points — located throughout the body at junctions of muscles, veins, ligaments, and bones. Stimulating these points releases blocked prana (life energy) and promotes profound healing.

Each session is uniquely tailored, often incorporating specialist oils and gentle massage alongside precise point work to enhance the therapeutic effect.
